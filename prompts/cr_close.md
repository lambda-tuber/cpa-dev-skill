# CR Close Phase

## Steps

1. Verify that all completion checklist items in `CR-XXXX_<title>/00_overview.md`
   are checked.
2. ⚠️ **Confirmation required**: Record the checklist results in
   `CR-XXXX_<title>/04_close.md`, then ask: "Tests are complete. May we close
   CR-XXXX?" — wait for approval before proceeding.
3. Update the status in `CR-XXXX_<title>/00_overview.md` to **Done**.
4. Update the CR list in the PB folder's `PB-STATUS.md` and set the CR status
   to **Done**.
